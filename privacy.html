Privacy Policy: Everest Chess 3D (offline-only version)

Effective date: October 27, 2026
Developer: Elias Utegaard (an individual developer)
Contact: support.utegaardgames@gmail.com

"We", "us" and "our" mean Elias Utegaard, the individual developer of Everest Chess 3D.

1. The short version

Everest Chess 3D doesn't collect any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app doesn't send anything over the internet.

2. What the app saves on your device

The app saves your settings (board, piece set, language, sounds, Everest's tips, "Read tips aloud"), your progress (games won against the computer, which unlocks boards and piece sets) and, if you type them, the two player names for two-player games (up to 20 characters each). It also keeps temporary files it can rebuild, such as prepared images of the pieces. All of this stays on your iPhone or iPad and isn't sent to us or anyone else.

3. What we don't do

  • No personal information or identifiers are collected, and we don't use your device's advertising identifier.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • The app has no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of its own. (Apple's optional analytics sharing is explained in section 4.)
  • No selling or sharing of data.
  • No third-party services or SDKs, and no code in the app that connects to the internet. The only link is to this policy, which opens in your browser.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

4. Third parties

The app shares no data with anyone, so no third party receives data from it. You buy the app through Apple's App Store; Apple handles payment under its privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and we don't receive your payment details.

App analytics from Apple. If you've turned on "Share With App Developers" (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us crash reports and statistics about how the app is used. Apple says it gives developers this information only when it is "aggregated or in a form that does not personally identify you." You can turn this off in the same setting at any time.

5. The chess engine and Everest's voice

The computer opponent (the open-source Arasan engine) runs entirely on your device. Everest speaks with pre-recorded, computer-generated (synthetic) audio clips built into the app. The app doesn't use your microphone. "Read tips aloud" (off by default) uses Apple's built-in speech synthesis on your device.

6. How long information is kept, and how to delete it

Data on your device stays until you change it or delete the app; deleting the app removes it. Device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer) are controlled by Apple and by you. Emails to us: see section 8.

7. Children

Everest Chess 3D is rated 4+, so people of any age, including children, may play it. It isn't in the App Store's Kids category. The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. It doesn't ask for ages or contact details, and it has no chat, accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or internet features. Player names typed for two-player games stay on the device. If we learn an email came from a child under 13, we'll use it only to reply once, won't share it or add it to any list, and will delete the email and our reply promptly after replying. Parents can email support.utegaardgames@gmail.com at any time to ask us to delete anything their child sent.

8. If you email us

If you email support.utegaardgames@gmail.com, we use your email address and message only to reply, and we'll delete the conversation if you ask. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail). We keep support emails only as long as we need them to help you, and no longer than 12 months.

9. Your privacy rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us whether we have personal information about you, ask for a copy, and ask us to correct or delete it. Privacy laws in some places, including California and other U.S. states, Brazil (the LGPD) and Mexico, give people these rights, and we offer them to everyone. The app collects none; the only personal information we might hold is emails you send us. We don't sell personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or use it to profile you. To make a request, email support.utegaardgames@gmail.com. We'll respond within 15 days, and we won't treat you differently for asking. If you're in Brazil, you can also complain to Brazil's data protection authority, the ANPD.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version at this same web address and update the effective date. If online play or any other data use is added, we'll update this policy before that version is released. The app doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and no other company collects information about you through the app for that purpose, so the app doesn't respond differently to "Do Not Track" signals. If we make a material change, we'll also mention it in the app's release notes.

11. Contact us

support.utegaardgames@gmail.com. Elias Utegaard

4. Terceros

La app no comparte datos con nadie, así que ningún tercero recibe datos de ella. Compras la app en el App Store de Apple; Apple se encarga del pago según su política de privacidad (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) y nosotros no recibimos tus datos de pago.

Analíticas de la app proporcionadas por Apple. Puedes desactivarlo en ese mismo ajuste en cualquier momento.

5. El motor de ajedrez y la voz de Everest

El rival de la computadora (el motor de código abierto Arasan) funciona por completo en tu dispositivo. Everest habla con clips de audio pregrabados y generados por computadora (sintéticos) incluidos en la app. La app no usa tu micrófono. "Leer consejos en voz alta" (desactivado de forma predeterminada) usa la síntesis de voz integrada de Apple en tu dispositivo.

Los datos en tu dispositivo se guardan hasta que los cambies o borres la app; al borrar la app se eliminan. Los respaldos del dispositivo (iCloud o computadora) los controlan Apple y tú. Correos que nos envías: ver la sección 8.

La app no recopila información personal de nadie, incluidos los niños. No pide edades ni datos de contacto, y no tiene chat, cuentas, anuncios, analíticas, rastreo ni funciones de internet. Los padres pueden escribir a support.utegaardgames@gmail.com en cualquier momento para pedirnos que borremos cualquier cosa que su hijo o hija nos haya enviado.

Si nos escribes a support.utegaardgames@gmail.com, usamos tu dirección y tu mensaje solo para responderte y borraremos la conversación si nos lo pides. Nuestro correo lo ofrece Google (Gmail). Guardamos los correos de soporte solo el tiempo necesario para ayudarte, y nunca más de 12 meses.

9. Tus derechos de privacidad

Vivas donde vivas, puedes preguntarnos si tenemos información personal sobre ti, pedir una copia y pedirnos que la corrijamos o la borremos. Las leyes de privacidad de algunos lugares, como California y otros estados de EE. UU., Brasil (la LGPD) y México, otorgan estos derechos, y nosotros los ofrecemos a todos. La app no recopila ninguna; la única información personal que podríamos tener son los correos que nos envías. No vendemos información personal, no la compartimos para publicidad dirigida y no la usamos para crear perfiles. Para hacer una solicitud, escribe a support.utegaardgames@gmail.com. Responderemos en un plazo de 15 días y no te trataremos de forma distinta por pedirlo. Si estás en Brasil, también puedes presentar una queja ante la autoridad de protección de datos de Brasil, la ANPD.

10. Cambios a esta política

Si cambiamos esta política, publicaremos la nueva versión en esta misma dirección web y actualizaremos la fecha.
