Privacy Policy

Everest Chess 3D
Effective date: October 23, 2026
Developer: Elias Utegaard

The short version

Everest Chess 3D does not collect any information about you. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn’t send anything over the internet. Everything the app saves stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

To work properly, the app saves a few things on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • Settings, such as your chosen board and pieces, and whether “Read tips aloud” is on.
  • Progress, such as games won and which boards and piece sets you’ve unlocked.

This information never leaves your device through the app. We (the developer) can’t see it, and it isn’t sent to us or to anyone else.

Deleting it: if you delete the app, iOS deletes these settings and this progress from that device.

Device backups: if you back up your device with iCloud Backup or to a computer, iOS may include the app’s saved progress and settings in that backup, as it does for most apps. Apple controls those backups under your Apple Account settings and Apple’s privacy policy, not ours. The app doesn’t send this data to us.

What we do not do

  • We don’t collect personal information: no name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or device or advertising identifiers.
  • We don’t use accounts or sign-in.
  • We don’t show ads, and the app contains no advertising code.
  • We don’t use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools from us or any third party.
  • We don’t sell, share or rent any information, because we have none to share.
  • There are no third-party services or SDKs in the app, and the app has no code that connects to the internet.

The chess engine and Everest’s voice

  • The computer opponent (the open-source Arasan chess engine) runs entirely on your device. Your moves aren’t sent anywhere.
  • Everest the red panda speaks with pre-recorded, computer-generated (synthetic) audio clips built into the app. The app does not use your microphone and does not record or listen to you.
  • Read tips aloud (off by default): if you turn this setting on, the app uses Apple’s built-in speech synthesis to read tips out loud. The text is spoken on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Children

Everest Chess 3D is not directed at children under 13, and it doesn’t collect personal information from anyone.

If you email us

If you choose to email us at support.utegaardgames@gmail.com, we’ll receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply to you. We won’t add you to a mailing list or share your email. Ask us any time and we’ll delete the conversation. The app itself still collects nothing.

Purchases

You buy the app through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy covers that purchase.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version at this same web address and update the effective date above. If a future version of the app starts collecting any data, for example if online play is added, we’ll update this policy before that version is released and describe exactly what is collected and why.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email support.utegaardgames@gmail.com.
